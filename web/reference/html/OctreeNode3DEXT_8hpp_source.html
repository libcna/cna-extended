4// CNA::Extended::World3DEXT::OctreeNode3DEXT -- new, non-upstream addition. See
5// 3d.md/plan3d.md at the repository root for the design.
6//
7// 3D counterpart of CNA::Extended::Collisions::QuadTree, same recursive-subdivision
8// algorithm extended from 4 quadrants to 8 octants (Split() divides NodeBounds around its
9// volumetric center into 8 sub-boxes instead of 4 sub-rectangles; everything else --
10// Insert's non-exclusive "insert into every overlapping child" recursion, Query's dirty-flag
11// dedup, Shake's BFS merge-back-down, Remove's leaf-only-then-let-the-data's-own-parents-set-
12// drive-real-removal pattern -- is identical in shape to QuadTree, just walking 8 children
13// instead of 4). This is the piece that finishes bringing SpatialHash3DEXT's own header
14// comment's "true recursive octree subdivision can be added later as a separate broadphase
15// implementation" promise to reality (Octree3DEXT.hpp is the public
16// ICollisionBroadphase3DEXT adapter built on top of this class, mirroring QuadTree/
17// QuadTreeSpace's own split between "pure recursive tree" and "broadphase-adapter/ownership
18// logic").
19//
20// Ownership model, mirroring QuadTree.hpp exactly: Children genuinely owns its child nodes
21// (std::vector<std::unique_ptr<OctreeNode3DEXT>>, 0 or exactly 8, never partial). Contents is
22// a non-owning System::Collections::Generic::HashSet<OctreeNodeData3DEXT*>: an
23// OctreeNodeData3DEXT is owned by the Octree3DEXT that created it, not by the tree nodes
24// that merely reference it, matching OctreeNodeData3DEXT.hpp's own rationale.
25//
26// protected visibility on Children/Contents/CurrentDepth/MaxDepth/MaxObjectsPerNode is kept
27// (matching QuadTree.hpp's own precedent), even though no subclass exists in this codebase
28// yet.
