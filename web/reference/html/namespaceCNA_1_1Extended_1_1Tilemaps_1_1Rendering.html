◆ RenderMode

enum class CNA::Extended::Tilemaps::Rendering::RenderMode
strong

Specifies how ungrouped layers are rendered.

Enumerator
Merged 

All ungrouped layers are merged into a single draw call. Typically faster for maps with multiple layers due to reduced draw call overhead.

Separate 

Each ungrouped layer is drawn individually. May be faster for maps with frequently changing tiles, as only modified layers need rebuilding. Use benchmarks to determine best mode for your use case.
