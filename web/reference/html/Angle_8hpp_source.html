11// Fidelity note: upstream's Equals(Angle)/CompareTo(Angle) are NOT `readonly` methods -- they
12// call WrapPositive() on `this` (mutating it in place) and on a *by-value copy* of the `other`
13// parameter. This is preserved exactly: Equals/CompareTo below are non-const and take `other` by
14// value, so calling either one has the side effect of wrapping this Angle's Radians into
15// (-pi, pi]. operator==/!= take both operands by value for the same reason (upstream's operator
16// overloads take Angle by value, not by ref). GetHashCode() does NOT call WrapPositive() first
17// (unlike Equals) -- an apparent inconsistency in upstream between its equality and hashing
18// contracts, preserved as-is rather than "fixed", since this is a faithful port. Also: upstream's
19// `WrapPositive()` ends with a literal `Radians = Radians;` self-assignment (a no-op with zero
20// observable effect); omitted here as dead code, not as a behavioral simplification.
