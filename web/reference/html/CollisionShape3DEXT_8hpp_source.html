7// 3D counterpart of CNA::Extended::CollisionShape2D: a tagged union wrapping one of cna's
8// real bounding-volume types (BoundingBox, BoundingSphere -- both already reused elsewhere
9// in World3DEXT, e.g. RenderSystem3DEXT's frustum culling), scoped down to those two kinds
10// rather than porting CollisionShape2D's full 5-kind set (see CollisionShapeKind3DEXT.hpp's
11// header comment for why). Intersects() delegates entirely to BoundingBox/BoundingSphere's
12// own real Intersects methods, matching CollisionShape2D's own delegation-only design.
13// TryGetCollision()'s minimum-translation-vector math (Box/Box, Sphere/Sphere, Box/Sphere)
14// is standard, well-known collision-resolution math (closest-point-on-box-to-sphere-center
15// for Box/Sphere; per-axis overlap for Box/Box; center-distance-minus-radii for
16// Sphere/Sphere) -- not adapted from any specific source, analogous to how CollisionShape2D
17// implements its own legacy penetration-vector helpers as private static methods rather
18// than delegating them elsewhere.
